## What must work

A player opens a campaign, receives only information their character can know, enters an editable intention, reviews any interpreted action and submits it. Core checks the actual authority, resources, selected rule and current state; it commits supported effects once, records an outcome, advances supported elapsed processes and allows the next decision. AI narration and dialogue use legitimate received information and committed results. Closing, retrying, restarting and restoring must retain accepted actions without repeating their effects or charges.

The same loop must support continuing play, including relevant household and institutional decisions through the existing owners. An unsupported rule, missing fact or unavailable provider has an explicit unresolved/unavailable result and preserves the player's intention. The first AI-assisted release is a bounded campaign slice; full C2 completion remains a separate obligation with its existing DEV IDs and acceptance cases.
