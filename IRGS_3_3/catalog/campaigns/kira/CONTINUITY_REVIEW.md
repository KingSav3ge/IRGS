# Kira continuity review

These are editorial questions, not adopted world changes. All supplied accounts remain available. 1342 A.A. is the written reference year; exact dates, relative shelf positions and world-distance scales for the six maps remain unestablished.

## 1. Kieran’s longevity

Kieran is 2342 years old; the Flatland Evalian entry gives a lifespan of 1000 years. His Plains identity may correspond to Flatland, but that mapping and any exception remain unconfirmed.

Is Kieran exceptional, is the species lifespan a typical age rather than a limit, or should an old value change?

- KIRA-S03 §3: “Age: 2342”

- KIRA-S04 §2: “Lifespan: 1,000 years”

## 2. Duration of the dwarven war

The national histories date the invasion to 80 A.A. and peace to 400 A.A.; Kieran’s biography calls the war 228 years long. The numbers cannot be used as one exact continuous war duration without an explanation.

Does 228 describe Kieran’s participation or one phase, or is a date or duration incorrect?

- KIRA-S08 §3: “In 80 A.A the Dwarves attacked”

- KIRA-S09 §1: “The war ended in 400 A.A”

- KIRA-S03 §16: “The war lasted 228 years”

## 3. The rebellion and separation dates
