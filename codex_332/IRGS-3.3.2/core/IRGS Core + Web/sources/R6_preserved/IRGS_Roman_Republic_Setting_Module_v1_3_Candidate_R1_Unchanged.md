## 2. Roman premise and creation

Default premise: a frontier teenager entering captivity/slavery, before assignment or sale. Exact origin, chosen identity, family, capture history, motives, ambitions and voluntary past remain player-supplied or explicitly delegated. A school is a possible buyer, never a guaranteed destination.

Build a rich dossier from supported facts: names used/imposed; age and established origin; embodiment/appearance/fit/handedness; particular work and discipline evidence; language comprehension, expression and literacy/register; injuries and provision; custody/standing; actual goods and permitted funds; relationships, knowledge and obligations. Assess only task-relevant uncertainties. There is no mandatory HP total, attribute array, iron collar, clothing kit, starting balance or secret possession.

Initialize a particular place/date, custodian and asserted basis, shelter/current provision, observable restrictions, communication, actual held/issued goods, nearby responsible actors and an imminent demand, assessment or transfer. Keep authoritative truth, character knowledge and unresolved detail distinct under Core. Generated world detail cannot author the player's response or chosen history. An observational estimate cannot constrain hidden truth.

Ordinary human functions need no invented measurement to exist; exceptional burdens and specialized techniques still need supported capacity and instruction. Use particular language functions where material. A familiar instruction may be understood without general fluency/literacy; copying need not imply comprehension.

## 3. Standing, custody and manumission

Roman position references the Core standing view, with independent fields: **freedom condition; civic standing; former-slave/patron relations; household dependence; transaction capacity/restrictions; occupation/terms; office/mandate; allegations/findings; jurisdiction; evidence; local recognition/dispute**. Fame, money, skill and occupation do not automatically change the others.

Custody records reference the person, custodian, claimed basis, location, restraint/guard arrangement, practical movement/access, provision/care controller, transfer claim, witnesses and pending demand. People remain K-ACTOR actors. Institutional claims concerning them do not create stock lots or a second person.

For sale/transfer establish asserted authority, buyer, actual terms, inspection/access, disclosures, payment, handover and recognition. Their occurrence times can differ. Sale does not establish the original claim lawful. Escape changes location/control; emancipation, discovery, reports and pursuit require their own causes. There is no empire-wide instantaneous wanted state. Actual physical coercion can constrain or relocate a person; it cannot manufacture assent, confession or belief.

**ROM-LEGAL-01:** bind the applicable dated authority/jurisdiction, claimant, evidence, procedure and particular consequence. If a necessary rule is unresolved, record a narrow provisional ruling with its scope, effect and review trigger before material commitment. Practical refusal can occur while legal validity remains disputed. No universal hearing, appeal, compensation, fair treatment or automatic debt-to-slavery route is selected.

Release uses Core procedure with Roman terms: **promise → eligible route/terms → required acts → effective field change → recognition → continuing obligations**. Physical release, removal of restraints, escape, release from a bout, career discharge and manumission remain distinct.

Retained dated candidates are *vindicta*—an act before an appropriate magistrate; *censu*—census enrollment; and *testamento*—a testamentary disposition effective through applicable succession. They inherit the full reference §6's research limits. They are not interchangeable instant actions or universal eligibility rules. Select actual access, authority, eligible parties, required acts/participants, timing, costs, evidence, changed fields and administered-property consequences for the case. Do not backdate later Augustan restrictions.

Schedule actual prerequisites and apply the bound result when they occur. A promise can be breached; no automatic rescue or restitution follows. Completed success cannot be silently cancelled to preserve an arena plot. Later corrections and institutional reversals use the selected Core's distinct procedures, not blanket historical rollback.

Use *infamia* only with a dated meaning and specified consequence; otherwise record the actual stigma/exclusion. Explain a new title/status through its local authority, obligations, eligibility and limits, without granting unrecorded assets, expertise or universal recognition.

## 4. Roman funds, work and households

**ROM-MONEY-01:** account in sestertii, with **1 denarius = 4 sestertii**. This selected relation changes accounting views, not the coins physically held, tender acceptance, exchange access or market prices. Preserve exact fractions where needed. Historical provenance remains full reference §7.

A *peculium* or administered fund records legal owner, controller, permitted uses, actual holdings, withdrawal/revocation conditions and claims. Credit “for the fighter” is not unrestricted property. An organizer pays the actual contracted recipient; payment to a *lanista*/school does not also credit the fighter. Individual allowance is a separate permitted transfer. Earned unpaid pay is a claim until actual settlement; promised release changes legal position only through its configured act.

ECO owns generic agreement, production, sales, claims, cash flow and management procedures. The Roman profile supplies counterparties, specifications, dated local quotes, permitted spending, tenure/access, liability, practical enforcement and actual customers. A provisional quote is this offer, not representative Roman economic data. Do not import fantasy prices, modern employment protections or universal agricultural yields/profit rates.

Work can be compulsory, paid, dependent, household or reciprocal. Bind controller, task, provision/pay, schedule, supervision, tools/access, hazards and exit constraints. Assignment is a demand, not a voluntary PC action. Present credible enforcement and the meaningful decision. An owner's insistence cannot supply a missing function or fill a production shortfall.

Estates, workshops, households and schools instantiate Core work/service dependencies with their particular qualified people, seasonal windows, station capacity, provision, maintenance and records. An administered business can continue through actual delegation. No compulsory relationship decay or absence catastrophe applies. Progression can include useful language, craft, care, property management, enforceable rights, family and administration.

**ROM-PLAN-01** is a compatibility alias for inherited Core autonomous-obligation behavior. Keep its provenance ID, not another Roman scheduler. NPCs may comply, evade, delegate, renegotiate or breach when their goals, received knowledge and means support it; absence creates an actual capacity gap.

## 8. Local wear and the simultaneous-contact clarification

**ROM-WEAR-01 / RRP-WEAR-1.1:** for a chronologically ordered hit, use pre-impact protection, then material consequences for later contact. Armor stress is `Threat + Force + Sunder − relevant Resistance`, floor 0; Sunder affects material, not flesh. Stress 1 adds a mark and a second advances one state; Stress 2 advances one state; Stress 3+ establishes at least Breach.

Local states Intact/Damage/Breach reduce base resistance **0/1/2**, floor 0. A state transition clears marks. At Breach retain none; further fault/destruction requires an actual supported mechanism, not endless subtraction. Repair changes only the named restored component/state after work/acceptance. The sequential selections are unchanged from 1.0.

**New simultaneous-batch selection:** gather the complete group of genuinely simultaneous contacts affecting the same authoritative local condition state. Compute each contact's stress from its applicable pre-impact channel resistance, then apply material changes as one atomic batch. Cut and Pierce contacts cannot use separate batches if they change the same condition record; separate batches are valid only for actually separate states. Different causal times retain chronological processing; a display round or review interval is not proof of simultaneity.

Let `s` be pre-state 0/1/2, `m` prior pending mark 0/1, `n1` the number of distinct Stress 1 contacts and `n2` the number of distinct Stress 2 contacts:

- Any Stress 3+ establishes Breach, with zero marks; separately assess supported further component faults.
- Otherwise `advances = n2 + floor((m + n1) / 2)`; `new state = min(2, s + advances)`.
- Any advance clears pending marks. Without an advance, retain `(m + n1) mod 2`; Breach always retains zero marks.

This deliberately absorbs an unpaired light mark into an advancing batch, matching the existing clear-on-transition rule. It is the selected ordinal aggregation convention, not a calibrated material-strength or energy model. Splitting a simultaneous group can change this abstraction's result and is therefore prohibited: three Stress 1 contacts together advance once and clear all marks; four advance twice. Preserve canonical unordered source membership and commit the complete batch once; no source event can be charged again under another batch identity. Late discovery affecting an already resolved group needs scoped Core correction, not silent reprocessing. Validate state 0–2, marks 0–1 and zero marks at Breach; invalid inherited records require explicit reconciliation. Saturation does not prevent separately supported further component failure. Real causal coupling needing a different physical mechanism requires an explicit compatible profile.

Shield stress needs actual shield contact; an unknown miss is not evidence of interception. Use `Threat + Force + Sunder − Structure`: ≤0 no automatic damage; 1 mark, second degrades one condition state; 2 one state; 3+ two states or supported structural failure. Consume included marks on degradation. For simultaneous contacts, apply the same count/clear convention to the shield's bounded declared condition ladder, with each Stress 3+ contributing two steps unless an actually supported failure supersedes it. Do not substitute armor resistance states for shield function/condition.

Core stocks/handling track actual projectiles through ready/carried, released, landed/recoverable and recovered/damaged/lost. No usable second copy appears through recovery or scale change.
