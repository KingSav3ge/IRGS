I. REGISTRY GOVERNANCE
1. Purpose
This registry provides a persistent reference for the named characters and principal animal appearing in A Scot’s Tale: Become a Legend.
It records individual identity, physical characteristics, established background, demonstrated abilities, relationships, responsibilities, possessions, knowledge and conditions relevant to the ongoing campaign.
It also maintains provisional capability assessments and visual-design references without allowing either to overwrite established gameplay.
2. Authority and evidence
Information must be classified according to its source and certainty.
Established: Explicit player direction or an event confirmed within the campaign.
Testimony: Information a character reported, without necessarily proving the reported event.
Inference: A conclusion reasonably supported by evidence but not independently verified.
Provisional: Suggested age, appearance, history or competency that has not been conclusively established.
Unknown: Information unavailable or deliberately unresolved.
Superseded: Earlier information replaced by a correction or later verified event.
Approved concept sheets govern accepted visual likenesses (the user explicitly treats all included artwork as approved). They do not automatically prove illustrated dialogue, possessions, accomplishments, personal heraldry, rank, office, pricing, or social relationships. An approved sheet may still be recommended for visual revision, especially if another character looks similar. All 37 designs remain approved pending a replacement.
Numerical proficiencies appearing in this registry are retained from the earlier provisional character-building framework. They are not newly awarded skill increases or automatic adoption of an IRGS ruleset.
3. Identifier policy
Every established character has a permanent AST identifier.
Names and aliases point to the same individual when confirmed.
Similarly named individuals must remain separate.
Unnamed or uncertain identities must not be merged without evidence.
Removed duplicate registry entries must not be reused casually.
Corrected guard identities:
AST-36 — Roger Kett.
AST-37 — Adam Lark.
These are the two guards previously carried as unnamed entrance retainers. The subsequent AST-38 and AST-39 designations were duplicates and are withdrawn.
Corrected horse name: Abel. Earlier references to Able refer to the same horse; the original art filename retains the obsolete spelling as an archival filename, not the horse’s name.
Title qualification: AST-35 appears as Sir Nicholas Rookwood on the approved illustration and was called “Sir” in an art-generation request; earlier supported investigation material calls him Master Nicholas Rookwood. The image depicts arms and a signet but no independent campaign event verifies knighthood, nobility or a formal office. Preserve this title discrepancy as open for explicit confirmation and use “Master Nicholas Rookwood” in the simulation registry until authoritative confirmation.
